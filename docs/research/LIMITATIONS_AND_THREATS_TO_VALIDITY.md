# Prospective limitations and validity threats

Selection/coverage: an authorized complaint corpus may exclude silent users, channels,
agencies and periods. Missing event dates and uneven support limit trends and alerts.
Construct validity: taxonomy/severity rubrics require actual domain review; complaint
counts alone do not measure service quality. Label provenance must remain explicit.

Internal validity: duplicate/group leakage, final-test tuning, unequal case difficulty,
participant learning/order effects and AI anchoring can bias comparisons. Preserve
frozen groups, counterbalanced allocation and separate independent gold.
Statistical validity: participant/case repetition, sparse critical classes, missing or
abandoned responses and insufficient participants limit precision; no equivalence
claim follows from nonsignificance. Prespecified analysis cannot manufacture support.

External validity: one student and future convenience volunteers cannot establish broad
user or agency representativeness. There are currently no confirmed independent
annotators, participants or supervisor. Genuine pretrained PhoBERT results are absent.
Synthetic engineering tests validate implementation only.

Ethics/privacy: public visibility is not use permission; screening misses PII; aggregate
query differencing and backup retention remain risks. No approval or consent is presumed.
