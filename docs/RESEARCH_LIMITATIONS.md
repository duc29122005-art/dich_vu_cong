# Research limitations and current blockers

The project has one independent student researcher, Dương Thị Hồng Vân (EEP002-K49,
BIT, UEH), with no supervisor, team, confirmed independent annotators or recruited
participants. No ethics approval, consent, authorized citizen corpus, approved gold,
genuine PhoBERT fine-tuning result or human experiment has been established.

- RQ1 corpus frequencies cannot estimate citizen-population prevalence or satisfaction.
  Initial real reporting covers approved adjudicated development records only,
  explicitly excluding protected test cases. Reviewed/predicted real adapters need
  verified provenance; changing the label-basis flag is not valid evidence.
- RQ2 consumes existing authorized STEP 11 manifests only. No genuine nine-run result
  exists. Synthetic metrics and tiny random Transformers cannot substitute for real
  research models. Selection and thresholds remain validation-only under STEP 11.
- RQ3 synthetic numerical/spike checks establish software behavior. Incident reference
  events, exposure, seasonality and actual users are absent. Fixed interpretation
  questions and single-item workload ratings do not validate real administrative utility.
- RQ4 needs independent gold, volunteers, consent and approved evaluated assistance.
  Researcher self-testing and mock suggestions support interface feasibility only.
  Coarse difficulty strata, convenience recruitment, learning, fatigue, carryover,
  missingness and low case counts may bias eventual results.
- Participant/case-group bootstrap intervals are approximate, with engineering support
  floors rather than demonstrated power. Marginal intervals are not simultaneous
  significance tests. No evidence of difference is not evidence of equivalence.
- Separate IDs do not establish separate people or content independence. Existing
  governance plus genuine steward/reviewer evidence must address aliases, related
  cases, duplicate source versions and blind gold production. No test labels are
  opened to automate that judgment.
- Append-only filesystem bundles/checksums detect corruption and accidental overwrite;
  privileged operators can replace files and evidence. Trusted coordinator accounts,
  protected evidence, OS permissions, encryption, backup controls and external review
  remain necessary. A CSV event is not proof of informed consent.
- Report artifacts are private research tables. They omit participant/case identifiers
  and text, but small cells/rare outcomes can still disclose information. Public export
  requires separate permission and human disclosure review; no export is automatic.

The current user-reported PostgreSQL verification is historical context. Verification
in any new run requires the existing explicit isolated test database configuration;
missing test credentials must be reported as NOT_VERIFIED for that run. STEP 15
introduces no database migration and never resets an existing database.

Before real work: obtain source/provider participant rights; establish applicable
institutional review; recruit actual independent gold reviewers and volunteers;
complete actual gold/pilot/data/model gates; finalize sample-size and material/clock
design; record consent, ethics/access and allocation approvals; then collect measured
observations. Do not treat technical PASS as scientific completion.

STEP 16 may test a synthetic end-to-end workflow after STEP 15 verification. It is
not implemented here. No new research approval follows from that readiness status.
