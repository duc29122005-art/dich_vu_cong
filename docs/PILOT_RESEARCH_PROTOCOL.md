# Proposed pilot research protocol

**Human approval pending.** STEP 07A validates software only. Real pilot sampling,
human agreement and research gold have not started. The master plan was unavailable;
this protocol follows STEP 07 requirements.

## Roles and prerequisites

The research owner approves protocol/taxonomy. A domain reviewer checks
administrative interpretation without inventing laws or SLAs. The custodian checks
source permission, annotation scope, retention and privacy. A coordinator verifies
evidence and imports submissions. Two trained annotators use distinct pseudonyms
and ordinary Windows accounts. A separate designated adjudicator reviews all rows.
Coordinator/admin access is trusted and must be audited.

Require exact taxonomy/handbook approval, current source permission, verified
published research data, mandatory quality/privacy gates, approved plan and at
least 200 eligible unique records. No synthetic-to-research promotion is allowed.

## Sampling

Target 200 unique records, seed 42, proportional stratified random selection by
available domain and known UTC month. Collapse exact duplicates; retain keyed
near-duplicate/same-case candidate groups. Sample without replacement. Missing
metadata stays unknown; inferred topics never determine quotas. Pin source,
taxonomy, algorithm, selection order, seed and quotas in the immutable manifest.

Population representativeness is not established. Sparse strata can receive zero
quota; source selection, privacy exclusions and missing metadata limit coverage.
A purposive method requires an amended protocol and explicitly versioned
implementation; it must not be called representative. Preserve related groups
for future split design. STEP 08 is not implemented.

## Training and independent labeling

Train both annotators on the approved handbook, severity evidence, negation,
multiple issues, exclusions and privacy escalation. Calibrate using a separate
set excluded from the pilot; document rule clarifications before independent work.
STEP 07A fabricates no calibration data or human scores.

Double-annotate **100% of the 200-record pilot**, with identical order and blank
labels. Annotators cannot view the other submission before independent receipt.
Prohibit discussion of specific pilot decisions during this phase. Preserve
timestamps, independent attestations, versioned corrections and agreed deadline.

For the full 2,000–5,000-record dataset, propose at least **20% double annotation**
(400–1,000 rows), plus targeted uncertain/rare cases. This planning target requires
owner approval; it is not a scientific guarantee. Select a coverage sample without
model-inferred labels; report purposive supplements separately. Full execution is
outside this step.

## Agreement, revision and final approval

Report separate nominal Kappa/raw agreement for all three tasks, comparable and
excluded counts, workflow disagreements, class distributions and confusion matrices.
Undefined Kappa stays null. Unknown severity is not a supervised class.
Use 0.70 only as a provisional review target. Low agreement triggers boundary
analysis and calibration, not automatic relabeling. High agreement alone is not validity.

Meaning changes require a new taxonomy version/checksum, human approval and an
explicit new/reannotated pilot with lineage. After correction deadline, the
adjudicator explicitly decides every row, including agreements, with justifications
and evidence-bound approval. Retain A/B originals. Unresolved rows never become gold.
Lock and verify final versions; downstream use/publication still obeys source terms.

## Privacy and research completion

Export only authorized sanitized fields. Return new privacy concerns for review.
Aggregate reports contain no feedback text, PII or private human identities.
Test account isolation before deployment. Protect storage, transfers and backups;
apply authorized retention/deletion policy. No encryption or secure deletion is claimed.

STEP 07B requires approved taxonomy, authorized reviewed real data, 200 selected
records, two actual independent submissions, three agreement reports, complete human
adjudication and a verified pilot with no unresolved rows. STEP 08 research work
remains blocked until these conditions and its own prerequisites are satisfied.
